A paper/repo pair is a combination of a research paper and its corresponding code repository. Each pair with progressive repo may have been annotated with 
a research decision trajectory, which contains a sequence of decisions made by a research team over time. Each decision documents a scientific modification of 
the proposed method, how it is evaluated (experiment), or its hyperparameters and components (ablation). Each decision is supported by evidences from commit histories in the code repository.
It serves as a historical record that can be analyzed to understand the evolution of research ideas and to learn how to do research from researchers' demonstration.
Workflow to extract decisions are in annotate_codex.py. 

A research trajectory is considered well-annotated if 
- its decision contents are readable and clear human language with precise wording, complete sentence structure, no vague or ambiguous terms
- its decisions are neither too broad or coarse nor oversplitting
- by going over the trajectory, one can understand the back-and-forth evolution of the research idea and the rationale behind each decision
- its "trajectory_insight" field is clear, readable, neither over-generalized nor overspecific, and provides a meaningful description or insight about the idea evolution

It is OK to have a lot of decisions, since a repo can have a long history of research evolution. The key is whether the decisions are well-annotated and whether the trajectory insight is meaningful.
